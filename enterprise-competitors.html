Action Plan

Where Botanie Soap Can Improve

Checked against Botanie Soap’s live website before writing these — each one flags whether relevant content already exists or is a genuine gap.

01
Existing content — retarget

Make the contract-manufacturing pages comparison-ready

Contract & Private-Label Manufacturing is the one cluster where all three brands compete in volume: Vanguard Soap leads with 25 citations to Botanie’s 14 and Bradford’s 11. Botanie already has /pages/soap-contract-manufacturing and a new post, “Contract Soap Manufacturer USA: What the First Six Months Look Like.” Add a spec table on the page (1,000-bar and 100-gallon minimums per SKU, certifications, process steps), FAQ markup, and links from the post.

Why #1: it is the core buying-intent cluster, and the pages already exist, so closing a 11-citation gap needs structure rather than new copy.

02
Existing content — retarget

Consolidate the ingredients, organic and certification pages into one sourcing answer page

Botanie has zero citations in Ingredients, Sourcing & Sustainability while Bradford has 7 and Vanguard Soap 2. Its site already publishes /pages/ingredients, /pages/all-about-organic and /pages/certifications (USDA Organic, Oregon Tilth, Leaping Bunny, Vegan). Merging them into one page that answers sourcing and sustainability questions directly puts that proof where AI engines look.

Why #2: a zero-to-something opportunity built entirely from content that is already live.

03
Existing content — retarget

Package the bar soap content into a bar soap manufacturer page

Bar Soap / Beauty Bar is Bradford’s strongest cluster (14 citations to Vanguard Soap’s 3 and Botanie’s 0). Botanie has a wholesale natural soap collection plus posts on wholesale cold process soap and bar vs. liquid soap, but no page that targets the manufacturer query itself. Build one from that material.

Why #3: the largest competitor lead outside contract manufacturing, with source material available.

04
Genuine gap — new content

Add a dedicated custom formulation and syndet page

Custom Formulation & Syndet shows Bradford at 5 citations against 1 each for Botanie and Vanguard Soap. Botanie’s custom manufacturing page covers custom work broadly, and no separate formulation or syndet page surfaced in the site navigation. A page explaining the formulation process and syndet capability would give AI engines something specific to cite.

Why #4: a smaller cluster and genuinely new content, but a gap Vanguard Soap has not closed either.

05
Genuine gap — new content

Start a review-request cadence with recent B2B clients

Botanie holds the only verified rating in this comparison (Trustpilot 4.4 from 10 reviews), but just one review is from the past 12 months and Trustpilot flags no recent history of asking for reviews. Bradford and Vanguard Soap show none, so a steady flow of recent B2B reviews would widen a lead that is currently thin and ageing.

Why #5: brand reputation is 10% of the composite and the effort is low, but it moves the score less than the content fixes above.
